Common questions

I forgot my password / can't log in

Clients and staff are issued a login by their Buildo admin. If you've lost your password, email us at buildo1995@gmail.com or call us with your registered phone number, and we'll reset it for you.

How do I check my payment history or pending amount?

In the Client app, open Payments from the bottom navigation to see your total, paid and pending amounts, plus a full history with proof of each payment. You'll also get a notification whenever a new payment is recorded.

Where do I find my project documents and bills?

Open Documents to view drawings, agreements and site files, or Bills to view and download invoices. Tap any file to preview it, with an option to download.

Why haven't I received an update on my project?

Progress updates are posted by your project's admin or supervisor as work is completed. If it's been a while since your last update, reach out to us directly and we'll check in with your project team.

How do I update my phone number or email?

Go to Profile > More/Settings in the app, or contact us and we'll update it on your account.
