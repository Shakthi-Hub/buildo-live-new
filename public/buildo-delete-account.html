Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion directly from the Buildo app at any time. This page explains how to do it and what happens to your data.

Admin How to delete your Admin account

  1. Open the Buildo app and sign in as Admin.
  2. Tap the Profile icon or go to Admin Panel.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.
Immediate deletion. Admin accounts and all associated company data (projects, staff, leads, vendors, quotations, documents) are permanently deleted right away.

SupervisorClient How to delete your account

  1. Open the Buildo app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
Request sent to Admin. A deletion request is submitted to your company's Buildo Admin. Your account will be removed within 7 working days. You will receive an in-app notification once it is processed.

What data is deleted

Here's exactly what happens to each type of data once deletion is processed.

Account credentials
Permanently deleted
Profile information
Permanently deleted
Projects, payments, documents
Deleted with the account (Admin); transferred to Admin (Supervisor/Client)
Uploaded photos & files
Permanently deleted from the server
Push notification tokens
Permanently deleted

Need help?

If you are unable to delete your account from within the app, contact us and we will process your request manually within 7 working days.
