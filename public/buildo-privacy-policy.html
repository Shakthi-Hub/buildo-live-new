Privacy Policy

Effective: September 17, 2026  ·  Applies to the Buildo Client, Admin and Supervisor apps

Buildo's Client, Admin and Supervisor apps are how Buildo runs construction and interior design projects for its clients and team. This policy explains what information we collect when you use these apps, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Buildo ("we", "us") is based in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India, and is the entity responsible for the information described below.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run your project through the app.

Account details
Name, phone number, email address, and your Client ID or staff login when your account is created.
Project information
Project name, address, stage and progress updates, and the drawings, agreements and other documents added to your project.
Payment records
Quotation and invoice amounts, amounts paid and pending, payment dates, the payment method you record (cash, UPI, bank transfer), and any receipt or proof images uploaded.
Photos & site media
Site photos and images attached to a project by the admin or supervisor team.
Device & notifications
Device type and a notification token, used only to deliver in-app alerts (e.g. a payment update or new document).
App activity
Basic sign-in and usage activity, used for security and to keep the app working reliably.

We do not collect card or bank account numbers — payments are recorded as amounts and methods only, not processed through the app.

How we use it

  • To run your project: show progress, documents, quotations and payment status to the right people.
  • To notify you: send an in-app or push notification when a payment is recorded or a document is added.
  • To provide support: respond when you contact us about your account or a project.
  • To keep the app secure: detect misuse, verify logins, and maintain accurate records.

Who can see it

  • Clients see only their own project — its progress, documents, quotations and payments.
  • Admin staff can see all projects, staff, vendor and financial records needed to run the business.
  • Supervisors see the same project data as admin, except payment verification, expenses and profit & loss, which stay admin-only.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with outside parties for their own marketing.
  • We use trusted service providers only to operate the app itself — such as cloud hosting for documents and photos, and push-notification delivery — and only to the extent needed to provide that service.
  • We may disclose information if required to by law, or to protect Buildo's or a user's rights and safety.

Storage & security

Project documents, photos and records are stored on secured cloud infrastructure. Access within Buildo is limited by role — a supervisor cannot see what an admin sees, and a client can only see their own project. Passwords are never stored in plain text. As with any online service, no method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access.

How long we keep it

We keep project and payment records for as long as your project is active and for a reasonable period after completion, for record-keeping and legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your account data once a project is fully closed and there's no ongoing requirement to retain it.

Your choices

Access

Ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correct

Ask us to fix inaccurate account or contact details.

Delete

Ask us to delete your account and associated data, subject to records we're required to keep.

Notifications

Turn off push notifications any time from your device settings or the app's Notification Settings.

To exercise any of these, email us at the address below — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

BuildTrack is intended for adult clients, staff and business use. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
